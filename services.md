# Company Services — Service Information

## Overview

The company primarily offers two categories of services:

1. **Excavation / Dirtworks**
2. **Hauling / Trucking**

The company serves both **residential and commercial** customers. A major advantage is that the company can provide multiple services with its own excavation equipment and dump trucks, allowing it to handle projects that may require excavation, hauling, grading, demolition, or a combination of these.

---

# 1. Hauling / Trucking

Hauling is the simpler of the two main services.

The company uses its trucks to transport materials such as:

* Dirt
* Soil
* Rock
* Rock chips
* Other similar construction materials

### How It Works

A typical hauling job involves:

1. The company arrives at a location where material is available.
2. The truck/trailer is loaded with the required material.
3. The company transports the material to the customer's project site.
4. The material is unloaded at the destination.

Customers may hire the company because they need material transported regularly or because they do not have functioning trucks/equipment available to move it themselves.

### Typical Uses

Hauling can be used for projects requiring:

* Dirt delivery
* Soil transportation
* Rock transportation
* Removal or transportation of excess dirt/material
* Construction material transportation
* Ongoing material hauling

The website should present hauling/trucking as a straightforward service focused on **moving materials efficiently from one location to another**.

---

# 2. Excavation / Dirtworks

Excavation/Dirtworks encompasses a much broader range of work than hauling.

The company has excavation equipment such as excavators and skid steers, along with dump trucks, allowing it to perform many different types of site and earthwork projects.

Services include:

### Septic & Drain Fields

The company performs septic-related excavation and drain-field work.

A drain field is part of a septic system. Wastewater leaves the septic tank through piping and enters the drain field, where the wastewater is distributed through pipes and filtered through layers of rock, sand, and soil.

The company has experience installing drain fields, including completing multiple drain-field projects in a typical week.

Septic and drain-field work may include:

* Excavating for septic systems
* Drain-field installation
* Excavating trenches and areas for septic components
* Preparing the surrounding ground

---

### Grading

Grading involves shaping and leveling the ground to create the correct elevation and slope for a project.

The company uses laser equipment to ensure the ground reaches the required elevations and is properly leveled.

Grading can prepare a site for:

* Concrete
* Building projects
* Construction
* Other site-development work

---

### Site Preparation

The company can prepare land before construction begins.

Site preparation may involve:

* Clearing and preparing the work area
* Moving dirt and soil
* Leveling and grading
* Preparing areas for construction
* Coordinating excavation and hauling as part of the same project

---

### Demolition

Because the company already operates excavation equipment, skid steers, and dump trucks, it can also perform certain demolition-related work.

Demolition projects may require:

* Excavators
* Skid steers
* Dump trucks
* Material removal

The company's equipment and excavation capabilities allow it to handle demolition and the subsequent removal/hauling of materials.

---

# Residential & Commercial Work

The company works on both **residential and commercial projects**.

Residential work can include projects such as:

* Septic systems
* Drain fields
* Residential grading
* Site preparation
* Dirtwork
* Material hauling

Commercial work can include:

* Site preparation
* Grading
* Excavation
* Demolition
* Dirt and material hauling

---

# What Makes the Company Different

The company is capable of handling several parts of an earthwork project rather than specializing in only one task.

Because the company has both **excavation equipment and trucks**, it can combine services such as:

**Excavate → Load → Haul → Grade → Prepare Site**

This makes the company more flexible for projects where multiple types of work are required.

The company can be described as a versatile excavation and earthwork contractor capable of handling a variety of residential and commercial projects.

# Website Content Guidance

When creating the service pages:

* Create a dedicated **Excavation / Dirtworks** page.
* Create a dedicated **Hauling / Trucking** page.
* Clearly explain what each service provides.
* Emphasize residential and commercial capabilities.
* Use real project photos when available.
* Highlight septic/drain-field work, grading, site preparation, and demolition under Excavation/Dirtworks.
* Highlight dirt, soil, rock, and material transportation under Hauling.
* Emphasize that the company's equipment allows it to handle multiple parts of a project.
* Keep claims accurate and do not invent services that are not listed here.
* Use professional, straightforward language appropriate for a local excavation company.
* Include clear calls-to-action encouraging customers to request an estimate.
