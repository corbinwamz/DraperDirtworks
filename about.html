Our Story

Owner Bradlee Draper grew up working alongside his father in the family business, formerly known as Clark Draper Construction, established in 1997. After his father's passing, Bradlee left college to carry the company forward, continuing to serve the Boise, Idaho area with a focus on excavation and trucking.

Today the business is Draper Dirtworks, locally owned and operated out of Nampa, Idaho. We work in the Treasure Valley every day.

We do the excavation, the dirt hauling, and the trucking ourselves, so there's one person to call and one crew answering for how the job turns out.

We're licensed and insured, and we price jobs honestly.
