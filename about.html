Our Story

Owner Bradlee Draper grew up working alongside his father in the family business, formally known as Clark Draper Construction, established in 1997. After his father's passing, Bradlee left college to carry the company forward, continuing to serve the Boise, Idaho area with a focus on excavation and trucking.

Today, that same commitment lives on as Draper Dirtworks, a locally owned and operated excavation and trucking company based right here in Nampa, Idaho. We work in the Treasure Valley every day, not just when it's convenient.

From breaking ground on a new driveway to hauling the last load off a job site, we handle excavation, dirt hauling, and trucking with the same crew from start to finish, so you get one point of contact and one standard of work throughout your project.

We're licensed and insured, and we believe in straight answers and honest pricing, no surprises, no shortcuts.
