One Crew, Full-Scope Earthwork

Because we own our excavators, skid steers, and dump trucks, we handle a wide range of earthwork for homeowners and contractors: septic systems and drain fields, grading, site preparation, and demolition. Owning every piece of equipment means we can combine excavation, hauling, and grading into a single scope instead of coordinating multiple subcontractors.

What We Handle

Residential and commercial excavation work, from breaking ground to a finished, graded site.

Septic & Drain Fields

Excavation for septic systems and drain-field installation, including trenching and ground prep for the pipe, rock, and soil layers that filter wastewater.

Grading

Laser-guided grading brings your site to the correct elevation and slope, ready for concrete, a building pad, or general land development.

Site Preparation

Clearing the work area, moving dirt and soil, and leveling ground, coordinated with hauling so your site is construction-ready.

Demolition

Excavators, skid steers, and dump trucks on hand for demolition work, plus hauling away the resulting debris and material.
