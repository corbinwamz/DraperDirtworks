* Display authentic customer feedback.
* Preserve the meaning and wording of the reviews.
* Do not invent testimonials, names, ratings, or claims.
* Show the customer's first name and last name when provided.
* All listed reviews are 5-star reviews.
* The section should feel trustworthy, professional, and natural.
* Consider displaying 3–6 testimonials initially, with an option to show more if appropriate.
* Include a link/button to view the company's Google reviews if available.
